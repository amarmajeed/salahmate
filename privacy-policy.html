Privacy Policy for SalahMate

Last Updated: November 20, 2026

SalahMate respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the SalahMate application uses, how it is processed, why it is needed, and the choices available to you.

SalahMate provides Islamic features including prayer times, Adhan and Iqamah notifications, Quran reading, Qibla direction, Ramadan calendars and fasting tracking, Salah guides, Hajj and Umrah guides, Zakat calculations, and automatic mosque silent mode.

1. Information We Use

SalahMate may use the following information to provide and improve its features:

1.1 Location Information

With your permission, SalahMate may access your device's location to:

  • Calculate accurate prayer times based on your location.
  • Determine the Qibla direction.
  • Detect nearby saved mosques for automatic silent mode.
  • Detect when you enter or leave a saved mosque, where the mosque detection feature is enabled.

Location access may be used while the application is open and, where supported and separately permitted, while the application is running in the background.

You can also manually select your city and location instead of using automatic location detection.

1.2 Notifications

With your permission, SalahMate uses notifications to provide:

  • Adhan alerts.
  • Iqamah reminders.
  • Prayer-time notifications.
  • Other reminders you choose to enable.

You can disable notifications through your device settings.

1.3 Device Permissions and Silent Mode

SalahMate may request Android Do Not Disturb or notification policy access to manage your phone's sound mode automatically.

If you enable Mosque Silent Mode, SalahMate may use location detection to activate silent mode when you enter a saved mosque and restore the previous or configured sound mode when you leave.

This permission is used for the app's sound-management functionality. SalahMate does not use it to access your calls, messages, or conversations.

1.4 Network Access

Internet access may be required to retrieve prayer times, synchronize subscription status, load necessary information, and display advertisements.

When you use automatic prayer-time calculations or online services, relevant location information or your manually selected city and country may be processed by the relevant service provider.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

SalahMate stores app preferences and feature data locally on your device where applicable. This may include:

  • Prayer-time preferences and calculation methods.
  • Selected location and saved mosque information.
  • Notification and sound settings.
  • Prayer tracking and completion records.
  • Quran bookmarks and reading preferences.
  • Ramadan fasting progress.
  • Zakat calculator inputs and results.
  • Other application settings and preferences.

SalahMate does not require you to create an account to use its core features.

The Quran, Salah, Hajj and Umrah guides, Ramadan information, and Zakat calculator may be used with locally stored content and calculations, depending on the feature.

3. Location and Background Location

Location access is important for prayer-time calculations and automatic mosque detection.

When you enable Mosque Silent Mode and grant the required permissions, SalahMate may use location information to determine whether you are near a saved mosque.

Depending on your device, Android version, app version, and granted permissions, location access may be available while the app is in use or in the background.

Location checks are intended to support the mosque detection feature and are designed to operate around relevant prayer-time periods rather than continuously tracking your movements.

Location information used for local mosque detection is compared with your saved mosque locations on your device.

You can disable location permissions or Mosque Silent Mode at any time through the application or your device settings. Disabling these permissions may affect prayer-time accuracy and automatic mosque detection.

4. Google AdMob and Advertising

SalahMate uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google AdMob and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as:

  • Advertising ID or other device identifiers.
  • IP address.
  • Device type, operating system, and technical information.
  • Advertising interactions and app usage information.
  • Diagnostic and performance information.
  • Approximate location information derived from IP address or other permitted signals.

This information may be used to provide, measure, and improve advertisements, prevent fraud, and support advertising services.

Advertising data is processed by Google and its partners according to their respective privacy policies and settings.

You can learn more about Google's privacy practices here:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

You may also be able to manage advertising personalization through your device's advertising settings.

5. Monthly and Yearly Subscriptions

SalahMate may offer optional paid subscription plans, including monthly and yearly subscriptions.

Subscriptions are processed through Google Play Billing on Android.

When you purchase a subscription:

  • Google Play processes the payment.
  • Google manages payment details and billing information.
  • SalahMate may receive subscription-related information, such as the selected subscription product, purchase status, expiry status, and transaction-related identifiers, to verify and provide access to subscription features.
  • SalahMate does not directly collect or store your full payment card details.

Subscription renewals, cancellations, payment methods, and refunds are managed through Google Play in accordance with Google's applicable terms and policies.

You can manage or cancel your subscription through your Google Play account.

Google Play's privacy information is available at:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Third-Party Services

SalahMate may rely on third-party services to provide certain functionality, including:

  • Google AdMob: Advertising services.
  • Google Play Billing: Subscription purchases and payment processing.
  • Prayer-time service providers: Retrieving prayer times and related calculation information.
  • Other technical service providers: Services necessary to operate, maintain, and improve the application.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

SalahMate does not control the independent privacy practices of third-party providers.

7. Data Sharing

SalahMate does not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party providers where necessary to provide services such as advertising, subscription verification, and online prayer-time calculations.

App preferences and feature data stored locally are not automatically uploaded to a SalahMate user account or central user database.

8. Data Security and Retention

SalahMate uses local device storage for application settings and supported feature data.

We do not maintain a central user database for ordinary app preferences or require users to register an account.

Information processed by third-party services, such as Google AdMob and Google Play Billing, is subject to the respective providers' retention and security practices.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. We therefore cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Deletion

SalahMate does not require an account for its core functionality.

You can remove locally stored SalahMate data by:

  • Clearing the application's storage and data through your device settings.
  • Uninstalling SalahMate from your device.

Clearing app data or uninstalling the application removes locally stored information, subject to your device's operating system and backup settings.

This does not necessarily delete information independently retained by Google AdMob, Google Play, or other third-party providers. Requests concerning information held by those providers should be directed to the relevant provider.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you may have rights to:

  • Request access to personal information processed about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of personal information where applicable.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Lodge a complaint with your relevant data protection authority.

To exercise applicable rights or ask questions about privacy, contact us using the details below.

11. Children's Privacy

SalahMate is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through account registration. If you believe that personal information has been collected from a child inappropriately, please contact us.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application features, third-party services, or legal requirements change.

Any updated version will be published with a revised "Last Updated" date. We encourage users to review this page periodically.
